## What are you supposed to do for the project
There are many projects, and each project has its own circumstances. However, everyone is expected to do the following:
1. Analyze: Start with analysis and literature review. Understand the method(s) and become comfortable with the subject. 
2. Reproduce: Implement/use the method(s) and assess their performance. 
    * (a) Try to reproduce claims in the literature. Craft test scenarios, use benchmarks, measure.
    * (b) Understand what parts are specific to the method. Change those parts and see if you can still reproduce the claims tested at (a).
3. Extend/Improve: Come up with a new test scenario, a new modification, an extension of the method which improves the method(s) or has high scientific value.

## How will the projects be graded:
1. Component 1: ongoing work before Christmas. 
2. Component 2: Oral Presentation (in Week 13 or Week 14)
3. Component 3: A project report

---

You will be evaluated based on:
* Decisions you have taken before seeing evidence. Decisions you have taken after seeing evidence.
* Your ablation skills (ablations are essential, but experiments only for the sake of having experiments are often useless).
* Quality of results. 
* Presenting skills.


---

Points will be deducted for:
* slop
* Defensive words
* Sentences/paragraphs with information gain 0
* Lack of ownership for both experimental decisions and results
* Verbosity

Points will be awarded for
* Clarity. Clear motivations, clear decisions, clear experiments, clear explanation of results.
* Nice visualizations
* Brevity (without forgetting to mention everything that needs to be mentioned)

The final grade will be based on the joint evaluation of component 3 and component 2, however component 1 is mandatory.


### Component 1: ongoing work before Christmas
You have to discuss about your project with the supervisor(s) and:
* (0) You have to provide a proof of analysis.
* (1) Which is a short report, in which you explain concisely the problem you are trying to solve or the method you are studying (1 paragraph).
* (2) You identify 1-2 research paper(s) that studies the same thing and summarize what it does (1 paragraph).
* (3) You detail both (1) and (2) in several paragraphs.
* (4) You decide what you want to do next based on your analysis (1-2 paragraphs).

### Component 3: A project report
You have to extend component 1 with the following aspects:
1. How did your understanding of the project theme changed while working on it?
2. What exactly did you reproduce?
    * (a) Try to reproduce claims in the literature. Craft test scenarios, use benchmarks, measure.`
    * (b) Understand what parts are specific to the method. Change those parts and see if you can still reproduce the claims tested at (a).
4. How did your understanding on the 1-2 research paper(s) changed while reproducing claims?
5. How did you extend or improve? Here you should have:
    * (a) A strong ablation study
    * (b) Good results
6. Leap in understanding: Choose a different problem/method, other than the one which was the focus of your project. Based on the progress from 1-5, apply what you learned, explain how to apply, and what is the result.
